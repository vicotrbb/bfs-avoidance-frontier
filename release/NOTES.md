# Research artifact v1.1.0

Revised preprint and complete reproducibility package for **Rigidity of Pattern-Avoiding Breadth-First Reading Words of Increasing Trees**, by Victor Bona.

The paper proves two structural results for every branching bound k: 312-avoiding realizability collapses to the complete k-ary heap shape, and 231-avoiding words of length 1 modulo k admit full k-ary realizations. Short parent-sequence arguments replace the earlier binary-only exposition. The revision also distinguishes the two 321 counterexample thresholds and derives exponential growth rate 4 from Defant's theorem.

Included artifacts:

- The complete 10-page article and LaTeX source.
- General-k Lean parent-sequence proofs and binary Lean inductive-tree proofs.
- Two enumeration programs, an independently checked degree-sequence recognizer, and reproduction commands.
- Six raw count tables, six indexed b-files, and the complete 47-word counterexample set.
- Full-table recomputation receipts, formal axiom audits, PDF inspection evidence, a release manifest, and detached checksums.

The sequence table contains 28 additional entries relative to the retained baseline, including one derived through the proved 231 identity. The long 312 b-files follow from the established Catalan recurrence and the proved heap identity.
